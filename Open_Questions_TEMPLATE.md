# Open questions

**This is the one place open questions live.** Not an application's status file, not a
handover note, not a chat transcript. Those point here; none keeps its own list. Parallel
lists diverge — on the corpus this toolkit was extracted from, four had already diverged
badly before this rule existed.

**Reader:** the candidate, and the orchestrating session. Drafting and review agents do not
need this file: every item in it is, by definition, a claim they must not make. What they need
is the rule at the fence in `Fact_Library.md` — everything in that file is confirmed, and
anything not in it is unlicensed.

**Answering an item is a two-file edit**: write the confirmed fact into `Fact_Library.md`
above the fence, then delete the entry here. An item that is answered but still listed is
worse than one nobody has asked — it keeps a licensed claim out of the documents.

**Silence here means nobody has asked.** It does not mean the candidate lacks the skill. The
agent should ask; only the **Recorded gaps** section of the fact library records a gap.

**Answers are not a one-way ratchet toward stronger claims.** Expect about half of them to
narrow a claim. That is the process working.
