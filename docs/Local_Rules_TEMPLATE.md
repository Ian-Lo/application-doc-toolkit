# Local rules

## Voice

Words, phrases and registers you prefer or want avoided in everything outgoing. For example:
words you never use, a formality level, spelling conventions, how you want to sound.

## Drafting

How the drafting role should adapt to your field. For example: which kinds of fact to lead
with, how long a summary should run, how your industry words its achievements.

## Review

What the review role should weigh more or less in your field. For example: a screener's
priorities in your industry, a section you want checked first.

## Recon

What company research should cover for the roles you apply for. For example: sources to check
first, topics that matter in your field, topics to skip.
